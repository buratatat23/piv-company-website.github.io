ABOUT PIV ID

What is Personal Identity Verification?

Personal Identity Verification (PIV ID) refers generally to a process used to check information about a person's identity. The exact steps, purpose, and level of assurance depend on the organization or platform conducting the check.

Some online services use identity checks as one part of their safety procedures. Before beginning any process, confirm which organization is requesting verification, which provider will handle your information, what the process costs (if anything), and how your data will be used.

YOUR PRIVACY & SECURITY

Understand how your information is handled.

Personal information deserves careful handling, especially during an identity-verification process. Before sharing details, review the provider's privacy policy and confirm what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, who may receive it, and how you can ask questions or request deletion where applicable.

Verification providers may use security measures such as encryption and access controls, but the protections available depend on the specific provider. Do not assume a service uses a particular safeguard unless its published documentation confirms it.

Where information is shared with an authorized verification provider or another service provider, review the applicable terms and privacy practices first. Only proceed when you understand and trust the process.

Before submitting sensitive details: confirm the request through the official website or support channel of the platform asking you to verify. IMPORTANT ONLINE DATING IDENTITY SECURITY NOTICE

Protecting your identity and personal information should always be a priority when meeting people through online dating platforms.

Beware of Unverified Date ID Services

Online searches may display different identity verification services offering dating ID cards at unusually low prices. However, a website appearing in Google Search does not automatically mean that it is legitimate, secure, or officially recognized.

Some unverified services may collect sensitive personal information, including identification documents, photographs, contact details, and other private information.

Before submitting your personal information or making a payment, always investigate the provider's identity, privacy policy, security practices, and refund conditions.

Protect Your Personal Information

  • Never submit identity documents to an unverified website.
  • Be careful with services requesting unexpected verification payments.
  • Read the privacy policy before sharing personal information.
  • Check whether the provider has a verifiable business identity and contact information.
  • Never assume that a dating ID is officially accepted by every dating platform.
  • Report suspected identity theft or misuse of personal information to the appropriate authorities.
