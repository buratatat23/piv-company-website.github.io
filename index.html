ABOUT PIV ID

What is Personal Identity Verification?

Personal Identity Verification (PIV ID) is a phrase that may be used to describe identity-checking processes. The meaning, standards, and legal recognition depend on the specific program and organization. The U.S. federal PIV credential is a particular government identity credential; other services should not imply that they are part of that program unless they can demonstrate authorization.

Some online services use identity checks as one part of their safety procedures. Before beginning any process, confirm which organization is requesting verification, which provider will handle your information, what the process costs (if anything), and how your data will be used.

This website provides general information and customer-support contact details. It does not itself certify identities or guarantee that a dating profile is genuine.

YOUR PRIVACY & SECURITY

Understand how your information is handled.

Personal information deserves careful handling, especially during an identity-verification process. Before sharing details, review the provider's privacy policy and confirm what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, who may receive it, and how you can ask questions or request deletion where applicable.

Verification providers may use security measures such as encryption and access controls, but the protections available depend on the specific provider. Do not assume a service uses a particular safeguard unless its published documentation confirms it.

Only proceed when you understand and trust the process. Do not send identity documents, passwords, one-time codes, or payment details through an unsolicited message or an unverified contact channel.

Before submitting sensitive details: confirm the request through the official website or support channel of the platform asking you to verify. IMPORTANT ONLINE DATING
IDENTITY SECURITY NOTICE

Protecting your identity and personal information should always be a priority when meeting people through online dating platforms.

Check identity-verification services carefully

A website appearing in a search engine does not automatically mean it is legitimate, secure, or officially recognized. Before using any service, investigate the provider's legal business identity, contact details, privacy policy, security practices, and refund conditions.

Some services may request sensitive information, including identification documents, photographs, and contact details. Share these only when you have verified who is collecting them and why they need them.

Protect your personal information

  • Use the official website or app of the dating platform to confirm whether verification is required.
  • Be cautious of unexpected fees, unusually low prices, or pressure to pay quickly.
  • Read the privacy policy and written refund terms before sharing information or paying.
  • Check whether the provider has a verifiable business identity and working contact information.
  • Do not assume that a dating platform is affiliated with or accepts a third-party ID service unless the platform confirms it directly.
