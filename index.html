ABOUT PIV ID

What is Personal Identity Verification?

Personal Identity Verification (PIV ID) refers generally to a process used to check information about a person's identity. The exact steps, purpose, and level of assurance depend on the organization or platform conducting the check.

Some online services use identity checks as one part of their safety procedures. Before beginning any process, confirm which organization is requesting verification, which provider will handle your information, what the process costs (if anything), and how your data will be used.

This website provides general information and customer-support contact details. It does not itself certify identities or guarantee that a dating profile is genuine.

YOUR PRIVACY & SECURITY

Understand how your information is handled.

Personal information deserves careful handling, especially during an identity-verification process. Before sharing details, review the provider's privacy policy and confirm what information is collected, why it is needed, how long it is retained, who may receive it, and how you can ask questions or request deletion where applicable.

Verification providers may use security measures such as encryption and access controls, but the protections available depend on the specific provider. Do not assume a service uses a particular safeguard unless its published documentation confirms it.

Where information is shared with an authorized verification provider or another service provider, review the applicable terms and privacy practices first. Only proceed when you understand and trust the process.

Before submitting sensitive details: confirm the request through the official website or support channel of the platform asking you to verify.
